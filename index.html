ABOUT RUN GUN RIOT

THE RIOT

An arcade battle royale built for 30, 40 and 50-combatant matches: eight districts, surface-to-sky traversal, underground routes, secrets, airdrops, destructible covers and the Crusher. Offline solo play additionally includes contracts, vendors and timed Riot Run courses — these stay on this device and are not part of the online alpha.

CREDITS

Game design, direction and production: G.W. Stevens. Engineering, systems support and original asset generation: collaborative AI production workflow. Built as an original independent game with no licensed characters or worlds.
